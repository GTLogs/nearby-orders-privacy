Near By Orders – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

Near By Orders is a local order-management tool for small businesses. It has no server, no user accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not transmit your data anywhere.

What information is stored

The app stores the following on your device only:

  • Order details you enter or paste: customer tower/wing, flat number, ordered items, quantities and delivery date/time
  • Products and bundles you configure: names, aliases, units and prices
  • Delivery and payment status, and any price adjustment you set for each order

All of this lives in a local database file on your phone. It is never uploaded, synced or shared with any server, company or third party.

Shared content (WhatsApp)

When you share a message into Near By Orders from WhatsApp, or paste text manually, the text is processed entirely on your device to extract order details. It is not sent anywhere and is not kept beyond what you choose to save as an order.

Permissions

The app can receive text shared from other apps. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or the internet.

Data deletion

Because all data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time by removing individual orders, products or bundles in the app, or by uninstalling the app, which permanently removes all stored data.

Third parties

Near By Orders does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising or data-collection services. No data is sold, rented or shared.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled (for example, cloud backup or export), this policy will be updated before that feature is released.
